Before continuing, review how Tracepoint handles incident data.

Purpose

Log files you provide are hashed, parsed and normalized into a searchable incident timeline and exportable report.

Where your data goes

The local app stores original files and case records on this device. The server uses a local evidence folder and SQLite; standalone mode uses browser storage. Nothing is sent to a remote service. Data is not encrypted, so protect the device and back up the database and evidence folder.

Use synthetic logs

For this local prototype, use synthetic or sanitized logs. Do not upload credentials, personal data or evidence you are not authorized to handle.

Prototype limits

Tracepoint supports incident triage. It does not acquire endpoints, remove malware, certify evidence or establish legal chain of custody.
